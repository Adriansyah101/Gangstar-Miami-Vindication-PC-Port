This folder is for your own music. Its songs play on a sixth radio station in the car.

To use it:
  1. Put your songs in this folder: mp3, ogg, flac, wav, m4a, aac or wma.
     Songs in subfolders are not played. On Linux, m4a, aac and wma need ffmpeg installed.
  2. The station is on by default. Custom Radio on the game's SOUND page, or CUSTOM_RADIO=0 in
     savegame\GangstarMV.ini, turns it off.
  3. In a car, switch to it like any other station.

The songs play in a shuffled order and follow the game's MUSIC volume. Custom Radio Volume on the
SOUND page sets how loud they are compared with that.

New songs are found when the station is turned on, and each time every song has played.

A release build's log does not name your songs, and the port sends nothing anywhere.
